#GCGo-livesUnassign.Ratio
Go-lives counted in the seller’s go-live week and attributed to the go-live GC/GM; unassignments counted in the unassignment week and attributed to the outgoing GC/GM (point-in-time, card 12438). Ratio is a mix indicator, not a per-cohort rate — the two events can fall in different weeks and a person can unassign sellers who went live under someone else. * = fewer than 10 go-lives attributed, so the ratio is not meaningful. Revival/pool GCs (who receive sellers after a pause) legitimately show many unassignments with ~0 go-lives — they sit at the top of the default sort by design, not as a performance signal.
